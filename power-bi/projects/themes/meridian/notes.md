# Meridian — modern

> Contemporary product-design flat UI. Cool neutrals, one confident indigo lead, colour used
> sparingly and on purpose. Generated by [`../_build-era-themes.py`](../_build-era-themes.py),
> which **fails the build** on any contrast, greyscale or colour-vision miss.

Part of the **era set** (fantasy → modern → future). Light and dark are twins, not cousins:
both modes are derived from the same hue angles, so `dataColors[i]` means the same thing in
either and a report can switch without re-authoring conditional formatting.

The most general-purpose theme in the set — reach for it when the report has no theme of its
own and you want it to look deliberate rather than default.

## Palette

| Slot | Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | indigo | `#4F44C9` | `#C5C1ED` |
| 1 | amber | `#8A691A` | `#D5A129` |
| 2 | teal | `#2A9085` | `#2FA095` |
| 3 | magenta | `#D46AD4` | `#C63AC6` |
| 4 | green · **good** | `#277046` | `#6BCB93` |
| 5 | rose · **bad** | `#CA4A6A` | `#DB8399` |
| 6 | azure | `#4C8FD2` | `#4289D0` |
| 7 | slate · **neutral** | `#464C59` | `#636C7E` |

Surfaces — light: cool page `#F5F7FA` on white cards, ink `#101623`.
Dark: near-black `#0D1016` page, panel `#161A21`, ink `#EDF0F5`.

**Slots 0–3 are the gated set.** Green and rose sit at 4–5: a red and a green in the gated
four is the single most common accessibility failure in a BI palette, and it is also the pair
people most want to use for good/bad. They are still here for exactly that — as the semantic
`good` / `bad` keys, where they are read one at a time rather than compared side by side.

## Measured

| | greyscale (min pair) | CVD (min pair) | smallest fill contrast | body text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| light | 1.26:1 | 0.236 | 3.08:1 | 16.9:1 |
| dark | 1.36:1 | 0.244 | 3.30:1 | 16.7:1 |

Gates: greyscale ≥ 1.25, CVD ≥ 0.18 (deuteranopia **and** protanopia, Viénot 1999), fills ≥ 3:1,
text ≥ 4.5:1 against **both** surfaces.

## Notes

- Ships `$schema` (`reportThemeSchema-2.152`), the full `textClasses` set, wildcard container
  defaults and filter-pane styling (`outspacePane` + `filterCard`).
- Container chrome is suppressed for `textbox` / `image` / `shape` / `actionButton`, and their
  padding zeroed — the wildcard padding clips a textbox used as a heading.
- `card` takes a reduced 4/4/8/8 padding; the wildcard 8/8/10/10 clips a caption on a small card.
- `slicer` styles `header` and `items` with `textSize`, **not** `fontSize` — `fontSize` is
  silently ignored on both containers.

Apply: see [the library README](../README.md#applying-to-a-report).
