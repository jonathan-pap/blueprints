# Hyperion — future

> Near-future instrument panel. Graphite and glass, electric cyan lead, high chroma held on a
> short leash. Generated by [`../_build-era-themes.py`](../_build-era-themes.py), which
> **fails the build** on any contrast, greyscale or colour-vision miss.

Part of the **era set** (fantasy → modern → future). Light and dark are twins, not cousins:
both modes are derived from the same hue angles, so `dataColors[i]` means the same thing in
either and a report can switch without re-authoring conditional formatting.

Hyperion **Dark** is the one to reach for — a luminous palette on graphite is the whole idea.
Hyperion Light exists so the same report can be printed or projected without changing its
identity, not because a near-future panel wants to be white.

## Measured

| | greyscale (min pair) | CVD (min pair) | smallest fill contrast | body text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| light | 1.26:1 | **0.187** | 3.08:1 | 17.5:1 |
| dark | 1.36:1 | 0.297 | 3.45:1 | 17.7:1 |

Gates: greyscale ≥ 1.25, CVD ≥ 0.18 (deuteranopia **and** protanopia, Viénot 1999), fills ≥ 3:1,
text ≥ 4.5:1 against **both** surfaces.

> **Hyperion Light has the thinnest CVD margin in the set — 0.187 against a 0.18 floor.** A
> sweep of the whole hue circle put the ceiling at 0.198 while cyan leads, so this is close to
> the best a cyan-led light theme can do: light mode caps every fill at 0.29 relative luminance
> to clear 3:1 on white, and dark colours compress under CVD simulation. If you need more
> headroom, use Meridian Light (0.236) or Hyperion **Dark** (0.297), whose ladder runs up to
> 0.56 and has room to spare.

## Notes

- Ships `$schema` (`reportThemeSchema-2.152`), the full `textClasses` set, wildcard container
  defaults and filter-pane styling (`outspacePane` + `filterCard`).
- Container chrome is suppressed for `textbox` / `image` / `shape` / `actionButton`, and their
  padding zeroed — the wildcard padding clips a textbox used as a heading.
- `card` takes a reduced 4/4/8/8 padding; the wildcard 8/8/10/10 clips a caption on a small card.
- `slicer` styles `header` and `items` with `textSize`, **not** `fontSize` — `fontSize` is
  silently ignored on both containers.

Apply: see [the library README](../README.md#applying-to-a-report).
