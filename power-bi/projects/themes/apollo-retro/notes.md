# Apollo Retro (Light) — design notes

Generated by [`../_build-space-themes.py`](../_build-space-themes.py). Edit the spec there and re-run;
do not hand-edit `apollo-retro-v1.0.json`.

## Intent

1960s agency print rather than screen. Cream stock canvas (`#F4F1EA`) with white cards floating on
it, navy ink (`#1C2333`), and the NASA "worm" orange as the hero. Editorial, not atmospheric.

The only light theme in the space family — pick it when the report gets exported to PDF or printed,
where the three dark themes turn into ink-heavy pages.

## Contrast — two colours were darkened

A light theme is where WCAG bites, and the build audit caught both cases:

- **`foregroundLight`** `#6B7280` → **`#656B7A`**. It carries the `dataTitle` text class; on the cream
  canvas the original sat at 4.29:1, just under AA. Now 4.7:1.
- **module gold** `#C99A2E` → **`#A67C1E`**. As a data fill on a white card it was 2.58:1, under the
  3:1 non-text threshold. Now 3.8:1.

Both are the classic light-theme traps — mid-tone grey text and yellow-family fills. Keep the audit
in `_build-space-themes.py` in place if you extend this palette.
